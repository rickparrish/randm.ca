TelnetDoor is a BBS door that SysOps can use to allow their users to remotely connect to other servers (of the SysOps choosing). It was quickly written by request, and I haven't really tested it much, but it seems to do the trick.

While the door is called TelnetDoor, it actually supports creating RLogin connections as well.
