What is R&M Software?
R&M Software is the name I have released my software under since February 21, 2004. Some people have asked what the letters R and M stand for, and there isn't much of a story behind it...they're just the initials of two nicknames I had in high-school (Ree and Manning).

Although R&M Software only came about in early 2004, I've been developing software for much longer, since 1995 or so. Previous to the R&M name I released my products under the MannSoft name (which was short for Manning's Software) at www.MannSoft.ca, so some of the older downloads on this site may still make reference to that defunct name/website.
